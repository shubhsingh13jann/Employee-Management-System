A 4-tier full-stack architecture coordinating corporate governance, strategic department planning, squad-level task delegation, and employee collaboration.

Execution, Blockers & Leaves

1 Distinct Powers: Manager vs. Supervisor

Dimension 👔 Tier 2: Manager (Strategic Lead) 👷 Tier 3: Supervisor (Operational Lead)
Authority Scope Departmental Macro-level (All squads/teams) Squad Micro-level (4 to 12 direct subordinates)
Project / Tasks Creates Projects, defines Milestones, sets budgets Breaks milestones into Task Tickets with deadlines & priorities
Leave Approvals Tier-2 / Escalated Leaves: Approves >3 days & annual leaves Tier-1 / Routine Leaves: Direct first-line sick/casual approvals
Performance Evaluates supervisor velocity & annual appraisals Monitors daily staff output, code quality, sprint completion
